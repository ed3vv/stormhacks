This is the exponential-map chart of the ground sphere. Distance from the centre is true metres and every straight line through the centre is a geodesic, but the whole world is the disk, and its entire rim is one point — the centre's antipode. The map stretches sideways as you approach that rim, so two props that look far apart out there are actually close together. The dashed ring is the equator, a quarter lap out, where things look largest from the centre.
